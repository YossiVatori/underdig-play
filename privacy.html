Privacy Policy for Underground Mine

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Underground Mine. The game is made by Yossi Vatori, an independent developer. If you have a question, write to yossivatori@gmail.com.

What the game keeps

The game saves your progress, such as your money, upgrades, land and settings, in storage on your own device. This save is not sent to the developer. The game has no accounts, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

Ads

The Android version shows optional ads that you choose to watch for a reward. These ads are provided by Unity Technologies through its Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads services. The game asks these services to treat every player as not having agreed to personalized ads and as having opted out of the sale of personal information. Unity's own systems still handle data in order to deliver ads.

Unity publishes the following list for its Unity Ads software. This data is collected by Unity and may be shared with its partners. It is not sent to the developer.

Unity's handling of this data is described in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

The web version of the game shows pretend ads only. They load no ad network and collect nothing.

Your choices

  • Watching an ad is always your choice. You can play without watching any.
  • You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your device's settings. On many phones this is under Settings, then Google, then Ads.
  • Some ads show a Data Privacy icon, where you can opt out of certain personalized ads.
  • To ask Unity to access or delete data it holds about you, follow the instructions in Unity's privacy policy, or write to DPO@unity3d.com.

Keeping and deleting data

Your save stays on your device until you delete it. Use START OVER in the game, or uninstall the game, to remove it. On the web version, clearing your browser's site data removes it. The developer holds no copy. Unity decides how long it keeps ad data, as described in its privacy policy.

Security

Your save is kept in the game's own storage on your device. Unity states that the data its ad software collects is encrypted while it travels over the internet.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, write to the address above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
